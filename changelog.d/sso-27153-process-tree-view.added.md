Process tree view (SSO-27153): a **Process Tree** checkbox on the Processes page nests each process under its parent, with collapse / expand per branch, on Linux and macOS. Branches open by default and a branch you close stays closed as new children appear; search keeps a match's ancestors visible, and processes whose parent isn't listed (other users' parents without **All Processes**, or exited parents) appear at the top level. Unchecking it returns to the Apps / Background grouping.
